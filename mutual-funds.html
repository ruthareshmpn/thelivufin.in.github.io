Mutual Funds

Pooled investments whose return, volatility and suitable holding period depend on the assets and objective of the selected scheme.

Understand the scheme first

Equity, debt and hybrid funds behave differently

Scheme-dependent risk

A mutual fund pools investor money and invests under a disclosed objective. Equity, debt, hybrid, index and other schemes have different volatility, credit exposure, costs and suitable holding periods.

Compare

Investment objective, portfolio, benchmark, Riskometer, expense ratio, exit load and tracking error where relevant.

SIP

A SIP is a contribution method, not a guarantee of profit or protection from market loss.

Direct vs regular

Direct plans avoid distributor commission but require the investor to select and monitor the scheme.
