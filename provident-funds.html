Long-term retirement saving

EPF, VPF and PPF

Three names, two systems: EPF and VPF share your workplace provident-fund account, while PPF is a separate 15-year small-savings account opened independently.

EPFO workplace savingGovernment small savingsLong-termTax rules apply
VPF is not a separate investment account.

VPF is the employee’s voluntary contribution above the normal EPF share. It generally earns the same declared EPF rate and follows the same withdrawal framework. The employer is not required to match this additional amount.

Access and withdrawal

When can the money be used?

EPF job change

Transfer the balance to the new employer-linked account so service continuity is preserved. A Universal Account Number helps consolidate member records.

EPF advances

Form 31 advances or partial withdrawals may be available for specified purposes such as illness, housing, marriage, education and approaching retirement, each with its own service and amount conditions.

EPF final settlement

Final withdrawal depends on retirement, unemployment and other permitted events. Tax treatment can differ when recognised-fund service is below five continuous years.

VPF access

Extra VPF does not create a freely redeemable deposit. Because it sits in EPF, the applicable EPF advance, transfer and settlement rules govern access.

PPF loan

A loan can be requested during the period specified by the PPF Scheme, based on an eligible earlier balance. Interest and repayment rules apply.

PPF partial withdrawal

One partial withdrawal per financial year is permitted after the prescribed waiting period, within the scheme’s balance-based ceiling. Premature closure is limited to stated grounds and may reduce interest.

Income tax

Contribution, interest and withdrawal tax treatment

Section 80COld tax regime benefit

Eligible employee EPF/VPF and PPF contributions can count within the combined ₹1.5 lakh Section 80C limit. The deduction is generally unavailable under the new tax regime.

High employee contribution₹2.5 lakh interest threshold

Where the employer also contributes, interest attributable to aggregate employee PF contributions above ₹2.5 lakh in a financial year is taxable under the prescribed calculation. This makes large VPF contributions important to review.

Employer contributionCombined ₹7.5 lakh rule

Aggregate employer contributions to recognised provident fund, NPS and approved superannuation fund above ₹7.5 lakh in a year can create a taxable perquisite, together with prescribed annual accretion.

EPF withdrawalFive-year service test

Recognised PF withdrawal after at least five years of continuous service is generally exempt. Earlier withdrawal can be taxable unless a specified exception applies; transferred past service can count.

PPF taxContribution, interest and maturity

Eligible contribution may qualify under Section 80C in the old regime. PPF interest and eligible maturity proceeds are exempt under current rules.

Tax recordsKeep separate yearly totals

Track mandatory EPF plus VPF as aggregate employee PF contribution, employer contributions and PPF deposits. Payroll, passbook and income-tax records should reconcile.

Tax caution: limits and treatment depend on the applicable tax year, employment history, recognised-fund status and chosen tax regime. This page is educational, not personalised tax advice.

Choosing between them

A practical decision sequence

  1. Preserve the employer-linked benefit. Understand the EPF/EPS split shown by payroll and verify deposits in the EPFO passbook.
  2. Use VPF only for genuinely long-term money. It can increase workplace retirement saving but reduces take-home pay and follows EPF access rules.
  3. Use PPF for an independent long-term bucket. It is not tied to employment and has its own deposit cap, 15-year term and extension rules.
  4. Check tax thresholds before increasing VPF. The ₹2.5 lakh employee-contribution threshold concerns interest taxability, while Section 80C has a separate combined ₹1.5 lakh deduction cap.
