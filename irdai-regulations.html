Insurance regulation and policyholder protection

IRDAI Regulations

The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India regulates insurers and insurance intermediaries and protects policyholder interests. Product terms still determine the actual cover and claim.

Regulatory scope

What comes under IRDAI

Insurance-specific authority
Insurers

Life, general, health and reinsurance companies operate within licence, solvency, governance and reporting requirements.

Insurance products

Product filing and applicable rules cover benefits, exclusions, pricing structures, surrender terms and customer disclosures.

Intermediaries

Agents, brokers, corporate agents, web aggregators, surveyors and other registered categories have role-specific duties.

Policy servicing

Rules address proposal information, policy documents, nomination, assignment, servicing, claims and grievance handling.

Before you buy

Verify the insurer, seller and policy

Registration is not a claim guarantee
  1. Identify the legal insurer. The app, bank or agent may only distribute the product.
  2. Verify the intermediary. Check its category and registration using IRDAI resources.
  3. Read the policy wording. Review exclusions, waiting periods, deductibles, sub-limits, surrender charges and renewal conditions.
  4. Use the free-look period where applicable. Check the policy's current terms and deadline rather than relying on a verbal promise.
  5. Keep proposal and medical disclosures accurate. Retain the submitted proposal, receipts and benefit illustration.

Boundary: IRDAI supervision does not make every insurance product an investment guarantee, and it does not require payment of a claim outside the policy terms.

Complaint route

Insurer first, then the formal escalation channels

Keep written evidence
  1. Write to the insurer's grievance officer. Include the policy or claim number and the remedy sought.
  2. Use Bima Bharosa. Register and track an unresolved or unsatisfactory grievance through the IRDAI system.
  3. Check Insurance Ombudsman eligibility. Eligible individual policyholder disputes may use the Ombudsman under its current rules and territorial jurisdiction.
  4. Use emergency channels for fraud. Secure accounts and notify the relevant bank, insurer and cybercrime/police channel immediately.
