Government-backed long-term saving

Public Provident Fund (PPF)

A separate 15-year small-savings account available through designated Post Offices and banks, with Government-notified interest and prescribed access rules.

Government backed₹500–₹1.5 lakh yearly15-year term
PPF is separate from EPF.

It is not linked to employment or EPFO. A resident individual opens and funds the account directly, subject to the Public Provident Fund Scheme, 2019.

How it works

PPF essentials

Who can open

A resident individual can open one account in their own name and may operate an account for a minor as guardian, subject to the combined deposit rules.

Annual deposit

Deposit at least ₹500 and no more than ₹1.5 lakh in a financial year. Deposits may be made in permitted instalments.

Maturity

The account matures after 15 years calculated from the end of the financial year in which it was opened.

Extension

After maturity, the account may be continued under the scheme, including five-year extension blocks with contribution when the required option is exercised.

Loan

Loans are available only in the prescribed early-year window and are limited by an eligible earlier balance, with repayment and interest conditions.

Partial withdrawal

One withdrawal per financial year is allowed after the prescribed waiting period, subject to the applicable percentage and balance test.

Closure and tax

Premature access and tax treatment

Premature closureRestricted grounds

Closure before maturity is permitted only after the required minimum period and for grounds specified by the scheme, such as serious illness, higher education or change in residency status. An interest reduction applies.

Death of subscriberNominee or legal heir claim

The account is closed under the scheme’s death-claim rules; it cannot simply continue as the deceased subscriber’s account.

Section 80COld-regime benefit

Eligible PPF deposits can count within the combined ₹1.5 lakh Section 80C deduction limit under the old tax regime.

Interest and maturityExempt under current rules

PPF interest and eligible maturity proceeds are exempt from income tax under current provisions.
