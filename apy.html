Government-guaranteed retirement income

Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

Contribute through a savings bank or Post Office savings account until age 60 and receive the selected guaranteed minimum monthly pension for life.

Govt backedBankPost OfficeRetirement
Important entry condition

New APY enrolment is for Indian citizens aged 18–40 who have a savings bank or Post Office savings account. Since 1 October 2022, a person who is or has been an income-tax payer on the application date cannot open a new APY account. Existing eligible accounts may continue if the subscriber later becomes a taxpayer.

How it works

APY details

Who can join

Indian citizens from age 18 through their 40th birthday, with a savings bank or Post Office savings account and who satisfy the income-tax-payer restriction at enrolment.

Pension choices

Select a guaranteed minimum pension of ₹1,000, ₹2,000, ₹3,000, ₹4,000 or ₹5,000 per month. A higher joining age or pension requires a higher contribution.

How contributions are paid

Contributions are auto-debited monthly, quarterly or half-yearly from the linked savings account. Keep enough balance available and clear overdue contributions with applicable interest.

Subscriber benefit

The selected guaranteed minimum pension begins at age 60 and continues for the subscriber’s lifetime.

Spouse benefit

After the subscriber’s death, the spouse receives the same guaranteed pension for life.

Nominee benefit

After both subscriber and spouse die, the accumulated pension wealth corresponding to the selected pension is returned to the nominee.

Before age 60

Exit, death and missed contributions

Voluntary exitPermitted under current rules

The subscriber generally receives their contributions plus net actual investment income after account-maintenance charges. Government co-contribution, if any, and its income are not returned in applicable cases.

Death before 60Spouse may continue or close

The spouse may continue contributions for the remaining period and receive pension from the subscriber’s age 60, or choose settlement under the applicable rules.

Delayed contributionsOverdues plus interest

The account is not automatically closed merely because a contribution was missed. Overdue contributions can be regularised with the prescribed interest, subject to available balance and scheme procedures.

Important: Contributions must continue until age 60 to receive the full guaranteed benefits. Use the associated bank or Post Office branch for pension and exit requests.

Income-tax treatment

Tax and eligibility are separate questions

PFRDA states that NPS tax benefits also apply to APY, subject to the Income-tax law and chosen tax regime. However, a person who is or has been an income-tax payer on the application date cannot open a new APY account.

Contribution deductionMay qualify under NPS deduction rules

Eligible APY contributions may fall under Section 80CCD deductions, including the additional ₹50,000 limit where applicable. Personal contribution deductions are generally associated with the old tax regime.

Later becoming a taxpayerExisting account can continue

An eligible subscriber who was not an income-tax payer when applying may continue APY if they become a taxpayer later.

Monthly pensionGenerally taxable when received

Pension income is generally included in taxable income in the year of receipt. Actual tax depends on total income and the law applicable at that time.

Keep recordsPRAN and contribution statements

Retain contribution statements, the linked-account records and pension documents for any deduction claim and future tax reporting.

Tax note: Do not open APY merely for a deduction without first checking the enrolment restriction. Tax rules and eligibility can change.
