Pensions and retirement accounts

PFRDA Regulations

The Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority regulates the National Pension System and other notified pension schemes, including the intermediaries that maintain, distribute and manage them.

Pension ecosystem

Who does what in NPS and APY

Roles are separated
Pension funds

Manage pension assets according to the selected scheme and permitted investment framework.

Central Recordkeeping Agencies

Maintain subscriber records, transactions and PRAN-linked account services.

Points of Presence

Provide subscriber-facing registration and transaction services within their authorised role.

NPS Trust and trustee bank

Have distinct oversight and fund-flow responsibilities within the architecture.

Custodian

Safekeeps scheme assets and supports settlement under the prescribed framework.

Annuity providers

Provide the annuity purchased at exit; the annuity contract then has its own insurer and terms.

Returns and access

Pension regulation is not a fixed-return promise

Market and rule risk
NPS returns

Returns depend on asset allocation, scheme choice, fund performance, fees and market conditions.

APY benefit

Contribution and pension rules depend on entry age and the selected pension under the notified scheme.

Partial withdrawals

Permitted purposes, limits and service conditions follow the current scheme rules.

Exit and annuity

Lump-sum and annuity requirements differ by exit type, corpus and current regulations.

Check current rules: pension withdrawal and tax treatment can change. Confirm current PFRDA and Income Tax guidance before acting.

Subscriber protection

Use the official grievance ladder

Escalate in sequence
  1. Level 1 — intermediary. Lodge the grievance on Pension Sahayak so it reaches the relevant intermediary or nodal office.
  2. Level 2 — NPS Trust. If unresolved within the stated period or the response is unsatisfactory, escalate through the portal.
  3. Level 3 — PFRDA Ombudsman. Eligible unresolved matters can be appealed using the official format.
  4. Further review. The framework provides revision to the designated PFRDA member and a final statutory appeal route.
