Investments and savings  /  Gold

Store of value and diversifier

Gold

Gold can diversify a portfolio, but it does not generate operating cash flow and its market value can rise or fall.

Ways to invest

Physical gold, ETFs and eligible funds

Price-linked

Gold can diversify a portfolio but produces no operating cash flow. Investors can hold physical gold or obtain price exposure through regulated Gold ETFs and eligible mutual-fund structures.

Physical gold

Includes making charges, purity verification, storage, insurance and a buy–sell spread.

Gold ETF

Trades on an exchange and tracks underlying gold, subject to expenses, tracking difference and market liquidity.

Main risk

Gold prices respond to global demand, currency moves, interest rates and geopolitical conditions.
