Other regulators  /  MCA

Companies, LLPs and statutory records

MCA Regulations

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs administers central corporate legislation through the MCA system, Registrars of Companies and other assigned bodies. MCA registration confirms corporate existence—not permission to offer every financial product.

Corporate administration

What MCA records and processes cover

Corporate-law scope
Incorporation

Formation and registration of companies and LLPs, including their legal names and identification numbers.

Directors and partners

Director identification and prescribed filings concerning directors, designated partners and key changes.

Annual and event filings

Financial statements, annual returns, registered-office changes, capital events and other statutory forms.

Charges

Prescribed filings can record security interests over company assets, subject to the meaning and currency of the public record.

Registrar action

Registrars process filings and exercise powers provided by corporate legislation and rules.

Corporate schemes

Specified mergers, arrangements, insolvency-related interfaces and other processes may also involve NCLT, IBBI, CCI or sector regulators.

Investor due diligence

How MCA information can help—and where it stops

Not a financial licence
  1. Search the exact legal name and CIN/LLPIN. Avoid relying only on a brand or website name.
  2. Review company master data. Check status, registered office, incorporation date and directors shown.
  3. Inspect available filings where relevant. Look for filing history, financial statements, annual returns and registered charges.
  4. Verify the sector licence separately. Banking, deposits, securities, insurance and pensions require the relevant RBI, SEBI, IRDAI or PFRDA authority.
  5. Assess the transaction itself. Incorporation does not prove solvency, authenticity, profitability or repayment ability.

Misleading claim: “MCA registered investment company” does not mean the investment is approved or guaranteed by the Government of India.

Help and complaints

Choose the route that matches the issue

MCA is not every investor forum
Portal or filing problem

Use the MCA help and service-request channels for account, payment, form or filing difficulties.

Corporate-law non-compliance

Use the prescribed MCA/Registrar process and provide the CIN, facts and supporting records.

Securities complaint

Listed securities, brokers and regulated market intermediaries normally require the SEBI route.

Fraud or crime

Urgent fraud may also need bank, cybercrime or police reporting rather than only a corporate filing complaint.
